# Testimonial Request Drafts — Quran Light Institute

> These drafts are for the CLIENT (the institute owner) to send to REAL parents
> on WhatsApp, to collect genuine testimonials. Nothing here is published on
> the website, and no testimonial is ever invented — a review appears on the
> Testimonials page only after a real parent replies in their own words and
> gives permission. Each draft asks for the same three things: name, country,
> and one line about the child's progress.

## Draft 1 — After the free trial (warm, short)

Classes at Quran Light Institute. If you are happy with the trial, would you
share a few words for our website? Just reply with:
1. Your name
2. Your country
3. One line about your child's progress

JazakAllah Khair!
